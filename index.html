Privacy Policy

Last updated: 25 July 2026

This Privacy Policy applies to the BuildYaar mobile application published under the package name com.buildyaar.app.

1. Introduction

BuildYaar (“BuildYaar,” “we,” “our,” or “us”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains how information is collected, used, stored, protected, and shared when you use the BuildYaar mobile application.

BuildYaar is a professional Bill of Quantities (“BOQ”) application designed to help plumbers, contractors, and construction professionals prepare plumbing material lists, manage quantities, generate BOQ PDF documents, and share those documents.

2. Information You Provide

Depending on how you use BuildYaar, you may voluntarily provide:

  • Your name.
  • Your firm or business name.
  • Your mobile number.
  • Products, variations, units, and quantities added to a BOQ.
  • BOQ records and generated BOQ document information.
  • Information included by you when contacting us for support.

BuildYaar does not currently require users to create an online account or password.

3. Information Stored on Your Device

Profile details and temporary cart or BOQ information may be stored locally on your device so that the application can provide its core functionality and remember information entered by you.

Removing the application or clearing its application data may delete information stored locally on the device.

4. Information Stored Using Firebase

BuildYaar uses Google Firebase infrastructure, including Cloud Firestore and Firebase Storage, to support application functionality. Depending on the feature being used, Firebase may process or store:

  • Profile information entered by the user.
  • BOQ order information.
  • Product and quantity information included in a BOQ.
  • Generated files or file references.
  • Technical information necessary to operate and secure the service.

Firebase is operated by Google. Information processed through Firebase is also subject to Google’s applicable privacy and security terms. Additional information is available on Firebase’s Privacy and Security page .

5. Automatically Processed Technical Information

The application and its service providers may automatically process limited technical information necessary to operate, secure, diagnose, and improve the application. This may include:

  • Device type and operating-system version.
  • Application version.
  • Internet and network request information.
  • Diagnostic, error, and performance information.
  • Technical identifiers generated by the application or Firebase.

BuildYaar does not use this information for behavioural advertising.

6. How We Use Information

We use information to:

  • Provide and operate BuildYaar’s features.
  • Save profile information entered by the user.
  • Create, format, and generate BOQ PDF documents.
  • Save BOQ records and associated product information.
  • Allow users to open and share generated BOQs.
  • Maintain the security and reliability of the application.
  • Diagnose errors and improve performance.
  • Respond to support and privacy requests.
  • Comply with applicable legal obligations.

7. PDF Creation and Sharing

BuildYaar allows users to generate BOQ PDF documents. A PDF may contain profile information entered by the user, such as the plumber’s name, firm name, mobile number, and BOQ product details.

When you choose to share a PDF, Android’s system sharing interface or a third-party application selected by you may process the document. Any third-party application you select operates under its own privacy policy and terms.

8. How We Share Information

We do not sell or rent your personal information.

Information may be processed or disclosed:

  • To service providers necessary to operate BuildYaar, including Google Firebase.
  • When you intentionally share a generated BOQ using another application.
  • When required by law, regulation, legal process, or a lawful government request.
  • When reasonably necessary to protect users, BuildYaar, or the public from fraud, abuse, security threats, or unlawful activity.

9. Advertising and Payments

BuildYaar does not currently display third-party advertisements.

BuildYaar does not currently process payments, bank details, card information, or in-app purchases.

10. Data Retention

Information may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the application’s functionality, maintain BOQ records, comply with applicable law, resolve disputes, enforce agreements, and protect the security of the application.

Information stored locally may remain on your device until you clear the application data or uninstall the application.

11. Data Deletion Requests

You may request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with you by emailing buildyaar@gmail.com.

Please include:

  • Your name.
  • Your registered or entered mobile number, if applicable.
  • A clear description of the information you want corrected or deleted.

We may request reasonable information to confirm that the request relates to you. We will respond within a reasonable period, subject to applicable legal, security, fraud-prevention, and record-retention requirements.

12. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative and technical measures designed to protect information from unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, misuse, or destruction.

No electronic storage system or internet transmission can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Therefore, we cannot guarantee absolute security.

13. Children’s Privacy

BuildYaar is intended for plumbers, contractors, businesses, and other professional users. It is not directed to children under 13 years of age, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13.

If you believe that a child has provided personal information through BuildYaar, please contact us so that we can review and delete it where appropriate.

14. International Processing

Service providers used by BuildYaar may process information on servers located outside your state, province, or country. Data-protection laws in those locations may differ from those in your location.

15. Your Choices and Rights

Depending on applicable law, you may have the right to request access, correction, deletion, restriction, or information about the processing of your personal information.

You may also choose not to enter optional profile information. However, some details may then be omitted from generated BOQ documents.

16. Third-Party Links and Services

BuildYaar may interact with third-party services, such as Google Firebase, Android system services, PDF viewers, messaging applications, email applications, or file-sharing applications.

We are not responsible for the privacy practices of third-party services. You should review their privacy policies before providing information to them.

17. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when the application, its features, applicable laws, or service providers change.

The revised policy will be published on this page with an updated “Last updated” date. Continued use of BuildYaar after an update means that the revised policy applies to your use of the application.
