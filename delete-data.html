Request Data Deletion

Last updated: 25 July 2026

This page explains how users of the BuildYaar mobile application can request deletion of their personal information and BOQ records.

How to Request Data Deletion

To request deletion of your data associated with BuildYaar, send an email to:

Email:
buildyaar@gmail.com

Please use the subject:

BuildYaar Data Deletion Request

Include the following information in your email:

  1. Your full name.
  2. Your firm or business name, if entered in the app.
  3. Your mobile number used or entered in BuildYaar.
  4. A clear request asking us to delete your BuildYaar data.

Data That May Be Deleted

After verifying your request, we may delete:

  • Your name.
  • Your firm or business name.
  • Your mobile number.
  • BOQ order records associated with your details.
  • Product, variation, unit, and quantity information in saved BOQs.
  • Generated BOQ files or associated file references stored by BuildYaar.

Data Stored on Your Device

Some profile, cart, or BOQ information may be stored locally on your device.

You can remove locally stored app data by:

  • Clearing BuildYaar’s application data from Android settings, or
  • Uninstalling the BuildYaar application.

Processing Time

We aim to review and process valid deletion requests within 30 days after receiving sufficient information to identify the relevant data.

We may contact you for additional information if reasonably necessary to verify that the deletion request relates to you.

Data That May Be Retained

Certain information may be retained when reasonably necessary for:

  • Compliance with applicable legal or regulatory obligations.
  • Fraud prevention and security.
  • Resolving disputes.
  • Enforcing legal agreements.
  • Maintaining limited backup records until routine deletion occurs.

Any retained information will be limited to what is reasonably necessary for those purposes and will not be used for advertising.

No Account Required

BuildYaar currently does not require users to create an online account. Data deletion requests are therefore matched using the information voluntarily entered in the application, such as name, firm name, mobile number, and BOQ details.

Need Help?
